Contact

Redlands Traditional Martial Arts is a home dojo, and admission is by invitation only.

Classes meet Monday and Wednesday evenings and are kept small so every student receives personal attention. If you would like to set up an introduction to learn more about our program, send Sensei Walker a message below.

To see what we’re up to, follow us on Facebook or watch our training on YouTube.

How to join

  1. Reach out. Send a message below to request an introduction.
  2. Introduction. We’ll talk about your goals, your experience, and what you’re looking for in training.
  3. Visits. If it seems like a good fit, you’ll be invited to attend a few classes, so we can both see whether the dojo is right for you.
  4. Invitation. If the fit is right, you’ll be invited to join.
