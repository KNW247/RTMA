🏆 Welcome to the California Homebrew Circuit (CHBC)

The California Homebrew Circuit is a grassroots initiative built by and for homebrewers across the Golden State. Our mission is to connect clubs and brewers through a network of local competitions, promote friendly rivalries, and a shared passion for the craft. Special thanks to our 2026 season sponsor, MoreBeer!, for providing an incredible prize package supporting the North, South, and All-State divisions.

🍻 How it Works

Compete in any competition listed in the Circuit. Your wins earn points on our leaderboard, helping highlight standout brewers from across California.

🎯 What CHBC Celebrates

CHBC exists to make homebrewing more connected, more fun, and more rewarding. By tracking competition results and highlighting standout performances, the circuit brings together the best of California’s homebrew scene—fostering community, friendly rivalry, and a little well-earned bragging rights along the way.
