Sensei Doug Walker

Sensei Walker began his training in 1985 as a direct student of Anthony C. Marquez Sr. and was one of the original students of Tanzenbukan Goju-Ryu, studying Okinawan Goju-Ryu and Kobudo for more than 35 years.

Today he leads Doshukan in Redlands, carrying that foundation forward with his own students and developing them into exceptional practitioners with an appreciation of the rich history of traditional Okinawan martial arts.
