Sensei Doug Walker

Sensei Walker began his training in 1985 as a direct student of Anthony C. Marquez Sr. and was one of the original students of Tanzenbukan Goju-Ryu, studying Okinawan Goju-Ryu and Kobudo for more than 35 years.

As a teen, Sensei Walker was fortunate to have the opportunity to meet Masanobu Shinjo, founder of Shobukan Goju-Ryu, and to test before him for 2nd kyu.

Today he leads Redlands Traditional Martial Arts and the Dōshūkan family, carrying that foundation forward with his own students and developing them into exceptional practitioners with an appreciation of the rich history of traditional Okinawan martial arts.
