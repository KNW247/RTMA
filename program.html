Our Program

Doshukan is a traditional, non-commercial dojo focusing on Okinawan Goju-Ryu Karate and Kobudo.

Goju-Ryu

Goju-Ryu (“hard-soft style”) is a traditional form of karate-do that features a combination of hard and soft techniques. It combines hard striking attacks such as kicks and punches with softer circular techniques for blocking and controlling the opponent, including locks, grappling, takedowns, and throws.

Particular emphasis is given to correct breathing and the refinement of fundamental techniques. Practice methods include kata (forms), body strengthening with traditional Okinawan weights, conditioning, kakie (sticking hands), partner drills, kumite (sparring), and self defense.

Ryukyu Kobudo

Kobudo, or Ryukyu Kobujutsu, refers to the martial arts weaponry systems originating on the island of Okinawa. Ryukyu Kobujutsu, literally “old martial way,” is the comprehensive study of the classical weapons traditions, including:

Bo
six-foot staff
Sai
metal truncheon
Tonfa
handled club
Nunchaku
flail
Jo
short staff
Eku
boat oar
Jutte-Bo
spear
Kama
sickle
Sansetsukon
three-section staff
Tinbe-Rochin
shield and spear

Training methods include kata (forms), the study of bunkai (practical application of kata), body strengthening, kihon waza (basic strikes and blocks combined with balanced transitioning), and partner drills.
