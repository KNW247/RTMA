Opening ceremony

  1. Head studentshugo line up
  2. Head studentki o tsuke attention
  3. Head studentseiza kneel
  4. Head studentmokuso meditation
  5. Head studentmokuso yame finish meditation
  6. Head studentshomen ni face front
  7. Head studentrei bow
  8. Head studentsensei ni face instructor
  9. Head studentrei bow
  10. Everybodyonegai shimasu “Will you please?” In karate: “Please teach me.”
  11. Head studentkiritsu stand up

Closing ceremony

  1. Head studentshugo line up
  2. Head studentki o tsuke attention
  3. Head studentseiza kneel
  4. Head studentmokuso meditation
  5. Head studentmokuso yame finish meditation
  6. Head studentdojo kun shouted by the head student, then by everyone
  7. Head studentshinjo ni face the shomen
  8. Head studentrei bow
  9. Head studentsensei ni face instructor
  10. Head studentrei bow
  11. Everybodyarigato gozaimashita “Thank you very much”
  12. Head studentotagai ni face each other
  13. Head studentrei bow
  14. Everybodyarigato gozaimashita “Thank you very much”
  15. Head studentkiritsu stand up
