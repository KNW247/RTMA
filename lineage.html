Sensei WalkerLineage

Lineage

Sensei Walker began training under Anthony C. Marquez Sr. in 1985, when Marquez was a senior instructor under Masanobu Shinjo and United States Director of Shobukan Goju-Ryu.

He was a student in Marquez’s dojo when it became Tanzenbukan Goju-Ryu, making him one of Tanzenbukan’s original students, and went on to train under Marquez for more than 35 years.

Marquez is the founder of Traditional Martial Arts International (TMAI), Tanzenbukan Goju-Ryu, and the Kokusai Ryukyu Kobujutsu Kenkyokai.

Roots on Okinawa

While stationed on Okinawa, Marquez was introduced to Masanobu Shinjo of the Shobukan Goju-Ryu Karate Organization. His first visit to Shinjo’s dojo led to a close relationship that included private lessons and an appointment as Shinjo’s adviser on international affairs. He received his 5th and 6th dan from Shinjo and became the Shobukan representative in the United States upon his return in 1985.

At the Shobukan Hombu, Marquez was introduced to Shinjo’s close friend Katsuyoshi Kanei of the Jinbukan Dojo, a Goju-Ryu master who specialized in Matayoshi-style Kobudo and was one of the leading seniors under Matayoshi Sensei. Marquez became one of Kanei’s private students, received Nidan at the Matayoshi Dojo in 1985, and served as Kanei’s United States representative until Kanei’s death in November 1993.

His search for advanced Kobudo training later returned him to Okinawa, where he trained under Kaisu Isa in the little-known system of Ufuchiku Kobujutsu, receiving a Renshi appointment in 1998.

The Dōshūkan family is grateful for the foundation built through this lineage.
