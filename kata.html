Kata

The traditional kata of Goju-Ryu karate.

Kata is the heart of traditional karate and kobudo. Through the continual study of kata, we unlock the meaning of our art and develop focus, power, proper posture, and balance. Where a kata is shown from two directions, the second angle helps when learning the turns.
